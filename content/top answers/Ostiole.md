An ***ostiole*** is a small hole or opening through which algae or fungi release their mature pores. The word is a diminutive of "ostium", "opening". The term is also used in higher plants, for example to denote the opening of the involuted syconium (fig inflorescence) through which fig wasps enter to pollinate and breed.
