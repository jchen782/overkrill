The **boab** (*Adansonia gregorii*) is a tree in the family Malvaceae, endemic to the northern regions of Western Australia and the Northern Territory of Australia. 

## Description
As with other baobabs, boabs are easily recognized by the swollen base of its trunk, which forms a massive caudex, giving the tree a bottle-like appearance. 
Boabs are deciduous, losing its leaves during the dry winter period and producing new leaves and large white flowers between December and May.

## Uses
Most parts of the tree are edible and it is the source of a number of materials. Its medicinal properties and the ability to store water through dry seasons has been exploited.
