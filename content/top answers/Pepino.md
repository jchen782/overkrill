The **pepino** (*Solanum muricatum*), which is also referred to as a pepino dulce or melon pear is a species of evergreen, frost-tender shrub that is native to the Andes and is known for its sweet edible fruit.
## Description
The fruit resembles a melon in color, and its flavor recalls a succulent mixture of honeydew and cucumber.

## Uses
Delicate and mild-flavored, pepinos are often eaten as a fresh snack fruit. Combine very well with a number of other fruits as well
